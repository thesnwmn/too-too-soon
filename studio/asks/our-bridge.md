If you say where (a place name, a road, a map link, or the stations either side), the studio can work out its timing points and the fraction along the track, test against that stretch, and hand you a link that opens straight onto your bridge.

Options:
- **Tell us** (recommended): a link to the spot, or a description.
- **Set it up yourself**: the page's set-up form (and later the map) does it.
