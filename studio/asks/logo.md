Three directions, drawn for `character` ([the sheet](../design/character/options-icons.png), [in a header](../design/character/options-headers.png)):

- **A, the engine** (shipped, recommended): a chunky yellow steam engine puffing white steam, on the green the site already uses. The word mark draws each "oo" as two driving wheels joined by a red coupling rod. Reads as a train even at 16 px in a browser tab.
- **B, the cloud**: "too too" written in a big steam cloud above a green engine, on sky blue. The most playful, but the words in the cloud are lost below about 64 px, so the tab icon is a blur.
- **C, the badge**: the engine in a round yellow-and-green badge on red, like an old enamel sign, with a spaced-out capitals word mark. Tidy, but more grown-up.

Recommendation: A. Say which, or what to change ("bigger puffs", "a red engine"), with `/director`.
