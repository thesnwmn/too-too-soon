## Learned
- RTT's live API sends times without offsets, contrary to its spec's wording: `rttTime` in core is the one way in (CLAUDE.md says so).
- Ask RTT's own error text: "token appears to be a token ID" solved the setup in one step.
- MapLibre 6's ESM build loads its worker by URL; a bundler needs `?worker&url` and `setWorkerUrl`.
- One `pnpm health --quick` went red at tier 4 and couldn't be reproduced in four runs since; watch for it.
